## 2. In short

- The playtest exists to find out how StrikeMania plays, so once you accept the notice the game shows when it starts, it records **playtest data**: how you play each level and how the game runs on your PC.
- This is **personal data**, not anonymous data: it is linked to your Steam account through our own IDs. We don't ask Steam for your name, profile, friends or other games.
- **Crash reports** are sent automatically, **once you have accepted the notice**. **Bug reports** are sent only when you press SEND.
- We don't sell your data and don't use it to target you with advertising. Our marketing team sees only totals, never one player's data.
- You can leave at any time (Settings ▸ DATA), and ask for a copy of your data or its deletion at [legal@strikemania.zone](mailto:legal@strikemania.zone).

## 3. What we collect

### 3.1 Playtest data

Once you accept the notice, the game records:

- **How you play:** the levels you open and how you reach them, each run you play (your inputs, the choices you make, how the run ends, your time), the thumbs up or down you give a level, and your personal bests.
- **How the game runs:** frame rate, the screen resolution, refresh rate and quality settings, and how a session ended (for example after a crash).
- **Your PC:** operating system, processor, memory, graphics card and its memory, whether you play on a Steam Deck or through Proton, and which kinds of controller are connected (for example Xbox, PlayStation or Steam Deck).
- **Where and how you play, once per launch:** your country as Steam estimates it from your IP address (a two-letter code), the offset of your PC's clock from UTC (not your time zone's name), the language you play in on Steam, your operating system's language, whether Steam runs in Big Picture mode, and when your Steam account got StrikeMania.
- **Which screens you open** in the game's menus. We don't record the values of your settings.
- **Our IDs:** an identifier for this installation, one for each session, and one for you as a player. They don't contain your name, but the player identifier is linked to your Steam account.

Nothing of this is read or recorded before you accept.

When the game crashes, it sends the game's log of that session the next time you start it, with known references to your Windows user name, your PC's name, your home folder and Steam IDs **removed**. A log can still contain other details from your PC.

The game keeps playtest data in files on your PC (section 8) until it has sent them.

### 3.2 Your Steam account

When the game connects to our servers, Steam gives it a login ticket for StrikeMania. Our server asks Steam to check that ticket, and Steam tells us your Steam account ID (your SteamID). We store the SteamID separately from the playtest records, and use it to recognise your account and keep your sessions together.

We never ask Steam for your name, profile, avatar, friends, Steam level or the other games you own. Valve tells game developers that you own the game. What Valve itself does with your Steam account is described in [Valve's privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).

### 3.3 Crash reports

Once you have accepted the notice, the game sends reports about crashes and errors to Sentry, our crash-reporting service. A report holds a description of the error and what led up to it in the game, your PC's hardware and operating system, and our player ID, which is linked to your Steam account. No screenshot is attached. A report may also include your PC's name and, for some crashes, the names of folders on your PC. Crash reports are kept 30 days.

### 3.4 Bug reports you send

When you press F1 (or choose to report a bug from a menu), you can write what went wrong and choose whether to include:

- a **screenshot** of the game's own window, taken when you opened the report, and
- the **game's log** of this session, with known references to your Windows user name, your PC's name, your home folder and Steam IDs **removed**.

Both are ticked when the report opens, and you can untick either. Nothing is sent until you press SEND. Your report also includes the moment of the run you were in, so we can see what happened.

Please don't write personal details in a bug report: we don't need them to fix a bug. A screenshot or a log can still show something personal by accident.

### 3.5 Leaderboard data (not active yet)

StrikeMania may have leaderboards, but **not during this playtest**. Before any leaderboard starts, we will update this policy and tell you in the game what it shows and to whom, and ask you where that needs your consent.

### 3.6 What we don't collect

We don't ask Steam for your profile, avatar, friends or other games, and we don't record your precise location, other programs running on your PC, or anything you type outside the bug-report form. We don't ask for your name or email address unless you write to us. Our servers use your IP address only in memory, to limit abuse, and don't store it.

## 4. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

| What | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Playtest data | To find and fix problems, balance and improve levels, see how the game runs on different PCs, and count, in totals per country and per hour of the day, where and when people play. Our marketing team sees only those totals | Your consent, given in the notice (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Crash reports | To find and fix crashes | Your consent, given in the notice (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Your Steam account | To confirm you are in the playtest, recognise you on later sessions and keep your records together | Necessary to run the playtest you take part in (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Bug reports and what you attach | To understand and fix the problem you report | Your consent, given when you press SEND (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Preventing abuse | To detect cheating and uploads that could harm our servers or other players' data. Our servers raise automatic alerts; a person looks at each before anything is done | Our legitimate interest in a fair game and safe servers (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

This playtest is a test of the game through its data, so taking part needs the playtest data and crash reports described above. You don't have to take part: if you don't accept the notice, the game closes without collecting playtest data or sending crash reports. You can stop at any time (section 7). Providing this data is not a legal obligation.

We don't make decisions about you based only on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects for you.

## 5. Who receives it

We use these service providers, which handle personal data on our behalf under processing contracts:

| Provider | What for | Where your data is |
|---|---|---|
| DigitalOcean | Our servers, database and file storage | the Netherlands (Amsterdam) and Germany (Frankfurt) |
| Cloudflare | An off-site copy of our backups | the EU |
| Sentry | Crash reports | the EU |
| Google (Google Workspace) | The email you send to legal@strikemania.zone | Google's data centres, which may be outside the EU |

Our team reviews bug reports in our own tools, which run on our servers, and team members, including the external developer, may download a report to their own computer to look at it. When a report becomes a task for the team, the task may quote what you wrote; those tasks are kept in our own issue tracker, which AlterEyes runs itself. Our team chat receives only daily totals (how many reports, for which levels), never what you wrote.

**Valve (Steam)** is not our provider: Valve is responsible for its own processing of your Steam account. We ask Valve to check your login ticket, and Valve tells us your SteamID.

We don't sell your data or give it to advertisers. We disclose it to authorities only when the law requires it.

## 6. Data outside the EU

Our servers and files are in the EU, and so are Sentry's crash reports. DigitalOcean, Cloudflare, Sentry and Google are companies from the United States: their authorised staff may access data from there, for example for support, and Sentry keeps its own account records in the United States. Valve is in the United States too.

For transfers to the United States, we rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified for it, and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us about these safeguards at [legal@strikemania.zone](mailto:legal@strikemania.zone).

## 7. Your choices and your rights

**Leaving.** To withdraw your consent and stop taking part, open **Settings ▸ DATA** in the game, open the data notice and choose **LEAVE PLAYTEST**. The game stops recording and sending crash reports, deletes what was still waiting on your PC to be sent, and closes; the next time you start it, it asks you again. Uninstalling the game also stops it. Leaving doesn't delete what we have already received: ask us for that (below).

**Your rights.** You can ask us:

- for access to your data, and a copy of the data you provided in a portable format;
- to correct it;
- to delete it;
- to restrict how we use it, or object to how we use it on the basis of our legitimate interest;
- and you can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Write to [legal@strikemania.zone](mailto:legal@strikemania.zone). To help us find your data, include your **StrikeMania ID**, which the game shows in **Settings ▸ DATA**, or a link to your Steam profile. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We answer within one month; if we need longer, we tell you why within that month.

When we delete your data, it is removed from our servers at once, together with the quotes of your bug reports in our team's tasks and the copies team members downloaded. Our backups expire within 30 days, and so do crash reports. If a backup ever has to be restored, your deletion is applied again. If you play again later, the game starts you as a new player.

**Questions or concerns?** Please write to us first, and we'll do our best to sort it out. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the one in the country where you live (in Belgium, the Data Protection Authority).

## 8. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Playtest data | up to 12 months after we receive it |
| What we work out from it (for example a run's result) | no longer than the playtest data it comes from |
| Bug-report text, screenshots and game logs, and copies or quotes of them in the team's tools | up to 6 months |
| Your Steam account ID, our player ID and their link | until 12 months after your last playtest session |
| Crash reports | 30 days |
| Our record of who on the team did what, and of deletion requests | 12 months |
| Backups | 30 days |
| Totals per level, country or hour that no longer identify anyone | may be kept |

**On your PC**, the game keeps files in `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\AlterEyes\StrikeMania` (on Linux or Steam Deck, in the game's Proton folder). They hold your settings, progress, saved replays and the playtest data waiting to be sent, which the game deletes once our servers have it. An upload our servers refuse is kept for 7 days, then deleted. LEAVE PLAYTEST deletes what waits to be sent; deleting the folder removes all of it.

## 9. How we protect it

Data travels to our servers encrypted. The playtest data waiting on your PC is encrypted too (screenshots and logs waiting to be sent are not). Our database and file storage are private and reachable only by our servers, copies of our database are encrypted, and our team needs a personal login to see any of it. The changes our team makes to players' data are logged.

## 10. Children

You need a Steam account to take part, so Steam's age rules apply (Steam accounts are for people aged 13 and over). Depending on the country where you live, if you are under 16 you may need a parent's or guardian's consent to take part; if so, please ask them before you accept the notice. If you believe a child has sent us data without the consent they needed, write to [legal@strikemania.zone](mailto:legal@strikemania.zone) and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

When we change how the game uses your data, we update this policy and its date. If a change needs your consent, the game shows its notice again and asks you before it collects anything for that new use.
